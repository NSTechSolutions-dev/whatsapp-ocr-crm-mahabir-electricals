When do automations run?

A simple picture of each active WhatsApp automation — what starts it, and what the customer receives.

How to read this: Each box is a step. Follow the arrows left to right. Every automation ends with a WhatsApp message to the customer.

1. Inquiry Follow-up

Runs about 3 days after a quotation is sent

If we sent a quotation and the customer has not replied, we gently follow up. 

flowchart LR
  A[Quotation sent
to customer] --> B{Did the customer
reply?}
  B -->|Yes| C[Do nothing]
  B -->|No, after 3 days| D[Send follow-up
WhatsApp message]
Customer gets: A message asking if they want to proceed with the quotation.

2. Price Drop Alert

Runs when a product’s price is lowered

If someone asked about a product recently (within about 30 days) and that product’s price goes down, we tell them. 

flowchart LR
  A[Product price
is reduced] --> B{Did a customer ask
about this product
in the last 30 days?}
  B -->|No| C[Do nothing]
  B -->|Yes| D[Send price-drop
WhatsApp message]
Customer gets: A message with the old price and the new lower price.

3. Repeat Engagement

Runs every day around 9:00 AM

For customers still open in the pipeline (Lead, Contacted, Proposal, or Negotiation) who have been quiet for about 30 days or more, we check in. 

flowchart LR
  A[Daily check
at 9 AM] --> B{Customer still
in open stage
and quiet for 30+ days?}
  B -->|No| C[Do nothing]
  B -->|Yes| D[Send check-in
WhatsApp message]
Customer gets: A friendly message inviting them to share a new requirement list.

4. Last Inquiry Reminder

Runs every day around 9:00 AM

If a quotation was sent about 7 or more days ago, the customer has not replied, and they are not Closed yet, we send a reminder. 

flowchart LR
  A[Daily check
at 9 AM] --> B{Old quotation
with no reply,
customer not Closed?}
  B -->|No| C[Do nothing]
  B -->|Yes| D[Send reminder
WhatsApp message]
Customer gets: A reminder about their last quotation.

5. Google Review Request

Runs when a customer is moved to Closed

When the deal is finished and the customer’s stage is set to Closed, we ask them for a Google review. 

flowchart LR
  A[Staff marks
customer as Closed] --> B[Send Google review
WhatsApp message]
Customer gets: A thank-you message with a request to leave a Google review.
